Quick answers

What dealers ask before they list with us.

Free: $0 monthly — our sales team handles buyer chats and you pay commission when you close. Premium: your own storefront, direct leads, lower commission, and priority buyer financing.
Premium: often the same day after Stripe checkout — you get portal access and can share your buyer link. Free: after we review and approve your application, usually within a few business days.
Buyer financing is a premium member benefit. We introduce qualified buyers to lending partners. When a deal is approved, you can be paid in full while the buyer repays monthly — terms depend on the partner and buyer profile.
Premium is $98/month with a 14-day free trial when you sign up by card through Stripe. Cancel during the trial if it is not the right fit. Commission on sales still applies, at a lower rate than the free tier.
Yes. Many dealers list on the free plan first, then move to premium when they want their own storefront, direct leads, and financing support. Message our team anytime and we will help you switch.
On free, our team replies to inquiries, answers availability questions, and notifies you when someone is ready to buy. On premium, leads come to you first — we stay in the background for backup and platform support.
Premium members get a branded dealer page with your inventory, photos, and a single link to share everywhere. Free listings appear in the hub; premium gives you a dedicated buyer destination.
A registered dealership or consistent car-selling business, a valid email, and a WhatsApp number we can reach you on. For premium, you will also complete secure checkout. We may ask for basic verification so buyers trust who they are buying from.
No lock-in contract. Premium is month-to-month after your trial. Free has no monthly fee — you only pay commission when our team helps you close a sale. You can pause or cancel premium through your account or by contacting support.
Stripe confirms payment, we provision your dealer ID and portal login, and you can start adding inventory. Check your email for next steps and your storefront link. Our team can walk you through your first listing if you want a hand.
